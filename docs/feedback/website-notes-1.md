### Rank insignia specs (sent to Josh)

- Five files, one per rank: `trainee`, `first-officer`, `captain`, `check-airman`, `chief-pilot`.
- SVG preferred. If raster: PNG, 512 × 512 px, transparent background.
- Square artboard, insignia centred, about 10% margin, the same visual weight across all five.
- Shown about 48–64 px tall beside each rank, so the design must still read at 48 px (avoid hairlines and small text).
- Must work on both the dark navy (#071724) and the cream (#fff9ee) backgrounds, or send a light and a dark version.

## Questions before the next round (as sent)

1. **Play-style buttons on the game pages.** The client asks for primary buttons such as "Play Tail Spotter
   Now", "Start Identifying Aircraft", "Find Your First Code" and "Take the Runway Challenge". The website rule is that
   it offers no play and the app is not released. Round 1 keeps "App release details". Options: use the
   client's labels and keep the link to release details, or ask the client for non-play labels.
2. **Wrong-looking button labels.** ATC Match's button reads "Take the Call Sign Challenge" (that is Call
   Sign Match). Every game page's second button reads "Be an Airport Spotter Share your Photos", including
   games that are not Airport Spotter. Round 1 keeps "Contribute content". Confirm the intended labels.
3. **Home third card.** The screenshot numbers the Military Aircraft Identifier card, but the text ("Every
   airport leaves a footprint. Study the runway layout…") describes Runway Identifier. Round 1 shows Runway
   Identifier there. Confirm.
4. **Missing copy.** No game-page copy for Aircraft Identifier (it keeps its current text). No games-page
   card copy for Airport Spotter: round 1 uses the client's homepage teaser for it.
5. **Pages 31–35 look swapped.** Page 32's text starts with Plane of the Day copy and then has the ranks
   copy; page 34's text starts with ranks copy and then Plane of the Day. Round 1 put each block on the page
   it describes. Page 32 also lists a "Your Photo Could Be Next!" button with a Plane of the Day line, used once
   on Plane of the Day. An "Explore the Archive" button was skipped because the archive is on the same page.
6. **Licence sentence on the photographer page** (page 37, item 4): "By submitting your photos, you grant
   Airplane Games permission to use, display, and feature your images … in accordance with our Terms and
   Conditions." This contradicts the current review conditions (a proposal grants no publishing licence, terms
   not final), and there is no Terms and Conditions page. Not applied. It needs the client's legal wording and
   the Terms page first.
7. **Image size for the photographer band** (page 46): the band image is cropped to fill the box, with the
   text over its left side. Supply about **2400 × 800 px** (3:1, minimum 1800 × 600), WebP or JPEG, with the
   subject on the right half.
